01 — About

A seasoned educator with a track record of improving student outcomes, developing curricula, and leading teams through change.

Odunayo has spent over two decades inside Nigerian and South African schools — first in the classroom, then running them. His work has always centred on the same question: what does this student, this staff room, this institution actually need to do better? That question took him from lesson plans to school-wide budgets, from supervising a classroom to reporting to a Governing Board.

He is now Administrative Manager at Arise and Shine Care Services, applying the same discipline — staff supervision, resource management, stakeholder liaison — to a new sector.
